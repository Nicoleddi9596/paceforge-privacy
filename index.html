PaceForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

PaceForge is an endurance training application currently being developed for runners, cyclists, swimmers, and multisport athletes.

1. Information We Collect

With your permission, PaceForge is designed to access fitness and health data including workouts, distance, pace, heart rate, sleep, recovery, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, and training strain.

2. Apple Health and WHOOP

PaceForge is developing integrations with Apple Health and WHOOP. These integrations require your authorization before accessing available data.

3. How Information Is Used

Authorized information is intended to help users track workouts, review training history, evaluate recovery, and monitor progress toward fitness goals.

4. Data Storage and Security

PaceForge is currently in development. The existing workout database uses local device storage. Planned synchronization, cloud storage, and security features will be documented before public release.

5. Third-Party Services

Planned integrations include Apple Health, WHOOP, and potentially other fitness providers. Data access is subject to provider authorization and applicable terms.

6. Data Control and Deletion

Users can revoke provider access through the relevant provider settings. Additional data management and deletion functionality is planned before public release. For privacy or deletion inquiries, contact us using the email address below.

7. Policy Updates

This policy will be updated as PaceForge introduces new functionality and data practices.
